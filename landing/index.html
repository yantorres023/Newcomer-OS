Erstmal

Your first 90 days in Germany, step by step.

For international students arriving in Germany: a personal checklist of what to do after you land, in which order and by when — with the official source for every step.

Independent app. Erstmal is not a government service and is not affiliated with any authority or university. It organises public information and is not legal advice. Always check the linked official source.

Who it's for

Students from outside the EU starting a degree or a preparatory course in Germany — for example students from India arriving for a Master's. It also shows a shorter list for visa-free nationals and EU citizens. City details for Berlin and Munich; other cities get the national rules plus official tools to find their local offices.

What it helps with

Register your address

The two-week Anmeldung and the landlord confirmation you need for it.

Residence permit

Apply before your visa ends, with your city's official application page.

Insurance & enrolment

Why your university needs proof of health insurance.

Letters you'll get

Tax ID, broadcasting fee, social insurance number: what they are and what to do.

How it works

  1. Answer a few questions: how you entered, where you'll live, a few dates. No name, no passport number, no account.
  2. Get your checklist: Today, Upcoming and Done.
  3. Open a task to see why it matters, what to bring, the steps, and the official page.
  4. Tick it off, add a private note, or set a reminder on your phone.

Official sources first

Every step links to where the rule actually comes from, and shows the date we last checked it. Our explanations are there to help you understand — if they ever differ from the official source, the official source is right. Rules are stored as structured, reviewable data, not generated by AI.

  • Federal law (gesetze-im-internet.de)
  • Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF)
  • Federal Foreign Office
  • Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt)
  • Federal Ministry of the Interior
  • Make it in Germany
  • Service Berlin
  • City of Munich
  • Rundfunkbeitrag (Beitragsservice)
  • Deutsche Rentenversicherung
  • DAAD

Private by design

No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your answers, progress and notes stay on your phone, and you can delete them at any time. The checklist works offline; only official pages need internet.
