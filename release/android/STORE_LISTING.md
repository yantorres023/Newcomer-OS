# Google Play — Store Listing Draft

**App name (≤30):** Erstmal: Germany arrival list
**Short description (≤80):** Your first steps as an international student in Germany, with official sources.

**Full description:**
Just arrived in Germany to study? Erstmal turns the first weeks of paperwork into a simple, personal checklist.

Answer a few questions — how you entered, where you live, a few dates — and see:
• What to do now, what comes next, and what is already done
• Your personal dates, e.g. for registering your address
• What you need to bring, step by step
• The official source for every step, with the date we last checked it
• Reminders on your phone (optional)

Covers address registration (Anmeldung), health insurance, university enrolment, the broadcasting fee, the student residence permit, your tax ID, work limits and more. City details for Berlin and Munich; official finder tools for other cities. English and German.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your answers stay on your phone.

Important: Erstmal is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or representing any government, authority or university. It organises public information and is not legal advice. Always check the linked official source.

Sources of information: German federal law (gesetze-im-internet.de), Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (bamf.de), Federal Foreign Office (auswaertiges-amt.de), Federal Central Tax Office (bzst.de), Federal Ministry of the Interior (bmi.bund.de, personalausweisportal.de), Make it in Germany (make-it-in-germany.com), Land Berlin (service.berlin.de), City of Munich (stadt.muenchen.de), ARD ZDF Deutschlandradio Beitragsservice (rundfunkbeitrag.de), Deutsche Rentenversicherung, DAAD.

## Policy notes (checked 2026-09-25)
- Google Play "Requirements for apps that communicate government information": non-affiliated apps must disclose sources clearly in the listing and state they don't represent a government → included above and in-app (welcome, settings, each source card).
- Misrepresentation policy update (effective ~mid-Aug 2026): no implied government affiliation → no government imagery; name not governmental.
- Do NOT use: "official", "government approved", "guaranteed", "never miss a legal requirement".
