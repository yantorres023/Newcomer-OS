## Summary
Erstmal works without an account and without a server. Everything you enter stays on your device. We (the publisher) do not receive, see or store your answers, progress, notes or reminders.

## What the app stores on your device
- Your answers used to build your checklist: how you entered Germany (category), degree or preparatory course, city (Berlin / Munich / other), arrival date, move-in date, visa end date, residence card expiry date, and yes/no answers (30 or older, plan to work, family joining).
- Which tasks you marked as done and when; your private notes; reminders you set; your language and notification settings.

Purpose: to show you the tasks and deadlines that fit your situation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — providing the service you request; processing happens only on your device).

## What we do not collect
No name, email, phone number, nationality, passport, visa, tax or ID numbers, photos, documents, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash reports. The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. By default they do not show task names on the lock screen; you can change this in Settings.

## Links to official websites
When you open an official source, your browser visits that website (e.g. a government portal). That website's own privacy policy applies.

## Backups
On Android, the app excludes its data from cloud backup and device transfer. On iOS, app data may be included in your device or iCloud backup according to your settings.

## Your control
- **Access/portability:** Settings → "Copy my data" copies all stored data (JSON).
- **Correction:** Settings → "Edit answers".
- **Deletion:** Settings → "Delete all my data", or uninstall the app.
Because we hold no data about you, requests to us cannot be fulfilled beyond this; you remain in full control on your device.
