- Creator/content sites for Indian students going to Germany dominate search results (e.g. msingermany.com, leapscholar.com, upgradgsp.com, thinkmile.in, avanse.com). Many monetise via loans/blocked accounts/insurance referrals (COMMUNITY/INFERENCE).
- University international offices publish arrival checklists (e.g. Augsburg "First steps for first semester students", KIT residence guide).
- Reddit r/germany, r/studyingermany; WhatsApp/Telegram batch groups (not observable from here).
- DAAD / study-in-germany.com community articles.

## Creator-outreach hypothesis
Mid-size creators (10k–200k) making "first week in Germany" content will feature a free, ad-free, sourced checklist because it makes their videos more useful and doesn't compete with their referral income. Test: 20 personalised emails → ≥ 3 features; measure installs via store listing campaign links (UTM on landing page only, no in-app tracking).

## Community-partnership hypothesis
International offices and student unions (AStA) in Berlin and Munich will link the app in orientation materials if it links their own pages and makes no commercial claims. Test: 10 offices → ≥ 2 pilots.

## Referral idea
"Share with your batch" button that shares the landing page URL (no personal data, no referral codes in V1). Measure via landing-page visits only.
