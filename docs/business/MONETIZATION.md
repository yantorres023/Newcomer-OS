# Monetization Research

_No pricing experiments or customer conversations have taken place. All options are hypotheses._

## Constraints
- The problem is temporary (~90 days; renewal every 1–3 years). A monthly subscription is a poor fit (INFERENCE).
- Students are price-sensitive but already pay: blocked-account set-up fees (Expatrio: €89 set-up from 1 Aug 2025 per its help centre), consultants/agents in India (COMMUNITY, amounts unverified).
- Trust is the product. Anything that makes a recommendation look bought damages it.

## Options
| Model | Fit | Pros | Cons / risks | Verdict |
|-------|-----|------|--------------|---------|
| Subscription | Low | Recurring | Problem is short; churn after 90 days; feels exploitative for a checklist | ✗ Not for V1 |
| One-time "Arrival Pack" (€3–7) | Medium | Matches finite job; no ongoing billing | Low ARPU; store fees; free alternatives | Test via fake-door |
| Free core + paid city packs | Low-Medium | Scales with coverage | Charging for the legal basics feels wrong; city data is the maintenance cost | Maybe later |
| Family pack | Low | Families have more tasks | Family rules need individual advice (legal risk) | ✗ V1 |
| Annual document/permit tracker | Medium (year 2+) | Renewal & work-day tracking is recurring | Small annual value | Test after V1 |
| Premium update alerts | Low-Medium | Rule-change alerts are real value | Requires backend + monitoring; implies obligation to be complete | Later |
| **University / student-union licence (B2B2C)** | **High (hypothesis)** | International offices want fewer repetitive questions and on-time permits; free for students; aligns incentives | Slow procurement; GDPR/DPAs (easier because no data leaves device); needs white-label/city packs | **Primary hypothesis** |
| Relocation partnerships (employers, agencies) | Medium | Budget exists | Pulls toward skilled-worker corridor; custom work | Later (corridor G) |
| Affiliate (banks, insurers, blocked accounts) | Revenue yes | Common in market (Expatrio-style bundles) | **Conflict of interest**: users would reasonably suspect recommendations are paid; insurance choice has legal/financial consequences | ✗ V1. If ever: separate "offers" area, clearly labelled, never inside task steps, never ranked by commission, disclosed per offer |

## Recommendation
1. V1 free, no ads, no affiliates (trust + store-review simplicity).
2. Validate B2B: offer 3–5 international offices a free pilot (their city pack + their links) in exchange for feedback and usage counts they collect themselves.
3. Fake-door test for a one-time Arrival Pack (e.g. printable/PDF checklist export, extra city packs) — measure taps, don't charge.
4. Revisit after one intake season with STOP/PIVOT gates in `VALIDATION_DEBT.md`.
