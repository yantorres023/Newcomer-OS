# Brand

## Recommended name: **Erstmal**
German colloquial for "first of all / for now". Signals "first steps in Germany", is friendly, easy to say in English, and does not sound like an authority.
- Conflict check (web search, 2026-09-25): no app found named "Erstmal". **Not** a trademark search — DPMA/EUIPO/USPTO search required (HUMAN_ACTION_REQUIRED).
- Store name suggestion: "Erstmal: Germany arrival checklist".

## Rejected / alternatives

## Tagline
- EN: "Your first 90 days in Germany, step by step."
- DE: "Ihre ersten 90 Tage in Deutschland, Schritt für Schritt."
- Supporting: "Every step linked to the official source."

## Tone
Calm, concrete, kind. Short sentences. Explain German terms, don't hide them. Never alarmist; never promise outcomes. "We" = the app team; authorities are named precisely.

## Visual direction
Teal-green seed colour (#1B6B5F), neutral surfaces, Material 3. Avoid black-red-gold, eagles, seals, stamps, flags, or official-looking document styling. Checklist and path metaphors.

## Icon brief
A simple rounded checkmark forming a path/arrow ("first step"), teal on off-white. No flag, no eagle, no passport, no government building. Must read at 48px.
