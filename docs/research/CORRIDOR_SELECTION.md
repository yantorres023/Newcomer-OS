# Corridor Selection

## ORIGIN / LANGUAGE COMMUNITY
Non-EU international students who need a national study visa for Germany. **Beachhead community: students from India** (largest and fastest-growing origin group per DAAD). The product itself is not India-specific: the rules apply to any non-EU student on a national (D) study visa.

## DESTINATION
Germany. V1 city packs: **Berlin** and **Munich** (official city service pages linked). All other cities get the federal roadmap plus official finder tools (BAMF-NAvI for the foreigners' authority).

## IMMIGRATION / ARRIVAL SITUATION
Arrived (or arriving within ~30 days) on a national study visa (or pre-study language course / Studienkolleg visa), enrolled or conditionally admitted at a German university, needs to convert the visa into a residence permit for studies (AufenthG §16b).

## PRIMARY USER
"Priya/Arjun" archetype (illustrative, not an interviewed person): 22–28, Master's student, English-medium programme, first time in Germany, limited German, smartphone-first, relies on YouTube/Instagram creators, WhatsApp/Telegram batch groups and Reddit (r/germany, r/studyingermany).

## WHY THIS CORRIDOR
- Highly repeatable, statute-backed sequence: register address (2 weeks) → tax ID arrives by post → health insurance proof → enrolment → residence permit before visa expiry → broadcasting-fee registration → work-day limits.
- Almost every rule has a primary source (federal law, BAMF, Auswärtiges Amt, BZSt, city service portals).
- Low need for individual eligibility judgement: the visa has already been granted; the app organises follow-up steps.
- Rules are comparatively stable (main recent change: 140/280 working days since 2024 Skilled Immigration Act reform).

## WHY NOW
- Record international enrolment in Germany (~402k in WS 2024/25) while Canada, UK, Australia restrict student inflows → students diverting to Germany (INFERENCE supported by Canada cap data).
- Winter semester intake (arrivals Aug–Oct) is a sharp, predictable seasonal trigger.

## MAIN PAINS (sources in EVIDENCE_LEDGER)
1. Not knowing the correct order (Anmeldung ↔ bank ↔ insurance ↔ residence permit).
2. Foreigners' office appointments scarce; fear of visa expiring before an appointment.
3. German-language terminology and forms.
4. Scattered, inconsistent advice (blogs disagree, e.g. "apply within 90 days" vs the actual rule "before your visa expires").
5. Letters arriving by post (tax ID, broadcasting fee) that newcomers don't expect.

## WHAT USERS CURRENTLY DO
University international-office checklists and orientation weeks; DAAD / study-in-germany.com; YouTube/Instagram creators; Reddit; WhatsApp/Telegram groups; commercial bundles (Expatrio, Fintiba, Coracle) that sell blocked accounts/insurance; paid consultants/agents in India.

## WHY EXISTING OPTIONS ARE INSUFFICIENT (HYPOTHESIS)
- University checklists are static PDFs/pages, not personalised to arrival date/visa expiry and not reminder-driven.
- Commercial apps are funnels for financial products (conflict of interest).
- Community advice is unsourced and often wrong on deadlines.
- None found combines: personalised deadlines + official source per step + offline + no account.

## MONETIZATION HYPOTHESIS
Free core for students. Revenue candidates: (1) B2B licence / white-label for university international offices and student unions; (2) optional one-time "Arrival Pack" unlock for extras (e.g. more city packs, document PDF export) — low price; (3) *no* paid referrals in V1 (conflict of interest). See `docs/business/MONETIZATION.md`.

## DISTRIBUTION HYPOTHESIS
Creators already producing "first steps in Germany" content for Indian students; university orientation weeks; student batch WhatsApp/Telegram groups; SEO for "Anmeldung within 14 days", "Fiktionsbescheinigung", "tax ID letter". See `docs/business/DISTRIBUTION.md`.

## LEGAL RISK
Medium-low. The app explains and links official steps; it never decides eligibility. Main risk: a user relying on a stale deadline. Mitigations: source + last-verified on every task, "verify with authority" copy, review workflow for rule changes, conservative deadline framing ("before your visa expires" rather than computed dates the law does not state).

## SOURCE QUALITY
High: federal statutes (gesetze-im-internet.de), BAMF, Auswärtiges Amt, BZSt, ARD ZDF Deutschlandradio Beitragsservice, Deutsche Rentenversicherung, service.berlin.de, stadt.muenchen.de, DAAD (publicly funded, not a government authority — labelled accordingly).

## LANGUAGES
- **English** — primary UI (the ICP's study language; Indian students are linguistically diverse, English is the shared language).
- **German** — full UI translation *and* official German terms displayed next to English explanations (users must recognise "Anmeldung", "Wohnungsgeberbestätigung" at the counter).
- Hindi/Telugu/Tamil deferred → `VALIDATION_DEBT.md`.
