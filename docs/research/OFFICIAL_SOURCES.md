## Claims relied upon

### `src.bmg.17`
- Whoever moves into a dwelling must register with the registration authority within two weeks of moving in.
- Whoever moves out of a dwelling and does not move into a new dwelling in Germany must deregister within two weeks of moving out.
- _Used by:_ `de.registration.anmeldung`, `de.registration.re_register_on_move`, `de.registration.deregister_on_leaving`

### `src.bmg.19`
- The landlord (Wohnungsgeber) must confirm the move-in in writing or electronically within the § 17(1) deadline.
- The confirmation contains landlord name/address, owner name if different, move-in date, address, names of persons registering.
- If the landlord does not provide it, the person registering must inform the registration authority without delay.
- _Used by:_ `de.housing.landlord_confirmation`

### `src.berlin.anmeldung`

- A Wohnungsgeberbestätigung and the registration form are required; appointment at a Bürgeramt, in person or by proxy.

### `src.berlin.study_permit`
- Granted for full-time studies at a state-recognised university; includes preparatory measures and compulsory internships.
- Generally granted and extended for two years; allows 140 full working days per calendar year.
- Enrolment or conditional admission is required.
- Apply online ('Application for a residence permit for language and study purposes'); the authority contacts you and may invite you to an in-person appointment with original documents.
- _Used by:_ `de.residence.permit_application`, `de.residence.permit_application_visa_free`, `de.residence.permit_renewal`

### `src.aufenthg.16b`
- Students from third countries may work 140 full days or 280 half days per year without approval of the Federal Employment Agency.
- Student auxiliary jobs (studentische Nebentätigkeiten) are not counted towards this limit.
- _Used by:_ `de.residence.permit_application`, `de.work.limits`

### `src.aufenthg.81`
- If an extension or another residence title is applied for before the current title expires, the current title is deemed to continue until the authority decides (para. 4).
- This does not apply to a Schengen visa under § 6(1).
- A certificate of this effect (Fiktionsbescheinigung) is issued.
- _Used by:_ `de.residence.permit_application`, `de.residence.fiktionsbescheinigung`, `de.residence.permit_renewal`
