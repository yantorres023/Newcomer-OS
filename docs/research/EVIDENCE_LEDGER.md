# Evidence Ledger

_No human interviews were conducted. Reddit could not be fetched directly from this environment; community evidence comes from search-indexed guides, university pages and commercial content. Every user-behaviour claim is therefore at most MEDIUM confidence and marked UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS where it concerns real user behaviour._

---

### A1 — Non-EU students in Germany face a repeatable post-arrival administrative sequence
- **SUPPORT:** BMG §17 (register within 2 weeks), BMG §19 (landlord confirmation), AufenthG §16b (study residence permit), §81(4) (fiction effect), BZSt (automatic tax ID), rundfunkbeitrag.de (one fee per dwelling), DAAD (insurance proof for enrolment). Berlin & Munich service pages describe the same steps.
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Local variation: Berlin uses an online pre-application then in-person appointment; Munich allows online/postal submission. Visa-free nationalities (e.g. USA, Brazil, Japan per Munich page) follow a different path.
- **OFFICIAL SOURCES:** gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__17.html, /__19.html; gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html, /__81.html; service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/120686, /305244; stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/studenten/1089339/; bzst.de IdNr pages.
- **COMMUNITY SOURCES:** msingermany.com "first things to do", expatrio.com arrival checklist, university guides (KIT, Northeastern NUin).
- **CONFIDENCE:** HIGH · **STATUS:** SUPPORTED
- **FALSIFY:** Evidence that a large share of students have steps handled entirely by their university (e.g. group residence-permit appointments) so there is nothing for them to track.

### A2 — Students are confused about order and deadlines
- **SUPPORT:** Guides repeatedly present "order of steps" as the key info; conflicting claims in the wild (e.g. "apply for residence permit within 90 days" in several guides vs the legal mechanism of applying before the visa expires; §81(4)). "Chicken and egg" Anmeldung↔bank discussions (expats.de, germanycompass.com).
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Universities run orientation weeks and provide checklists; many students may be well served.
- **CONFIDENCE:** MEDIUM · **STATUS:** UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS
- **FALSIFY:** Beta users complete tasks without opening task detail/source; qualitative feedback "I already knew this".

### A3 — Foreigners'-office appointment scarcity creates deadline anxiety
- **SUPPORT:** University guides: preliminary appointments "fully booked months ahead" (Northeastern NUin guide); Berlin page describes online application then invitation. Berlin fictional-certificate service page exists (service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/326233).
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Online pre-applications (Berlin, Munich) may reduce this; §81(4) protection reduces the real risk if applied in time.
- **CONFIDENCE:** MEDIUM · **STATUS:** SUPPORTED (as a public complaint) / UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS (as a product driver)
- **FALSIFY:** Beta users report getting appointments within days.

### A4 — A neutral, sourced, personalised checklist is missing
- **SUPPORT:** Competitors found are static guides (DAAD, blogs) or product funnels (Expatrio: blocked account + insurance "Value Package"; AbroadHub: community + jobs).
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** University international offices and study-in-germany.com are free and trusted; absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence (app-store search not possible from this environment).
- **CONFIDENCE:** LOW-MEDIUM · **STATUS:** WEAK
- **FALSIFY:** App-store review finds an existing well-rated neutral app with personalised deadlines.

### A5 — Students will trust an app that shows official sources
- **SUPPORT:** None direct. INFERENCE from general trust in government sources.
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Community (WhatsApp seniors, creators) may be trusted more than any app.
- **CONFIDENCE:** LOW · **STATUS:** UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS
- **FALSIFY:** <30% of beta users open any official source; trust survey below threshold.

### A6 — Students will pay
- **SUPPORT:** Students already pay agents/consultants and pay for bundles (Expatrio set-up fee €89 from Aug 2025 per Expatrio help centre).
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Tight budgets; free university support; problem lasts ~3 months (temporary).
- **CONFIDENCE:** LOW · **STATUS:** WEAK
- **FALSIFY:** Fake-door "Arrival Pack" conversion < 2% of activated users.

### A7 — B2B (universities) is a better payer than students
- **SUPPORT:** Universities have international offices with onboarding mandates; retention/drop-out concern (DAAD press release references drop-out rates).
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Slow procurement; data-protection scrutiny; they already have content.
- **CONFIDENCE:** LOW · **STATUS:** UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS
- **FALSIFY:** 0 of 10 international offices willing to pilot at no cost.

### A8 — Creator/community distribution is cheap for this ICP
- **SUPPORT:** Large volume of Indian-student-to-Germany content (search results dominated by msingermany, leapscholar, upgrad etc.).
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Many creators monetise via referrals to banks/insurers (would prefer affiliate partners).
- **CONFIDENCE:** MEDIUM · **STATUS:** UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS
- **FALSIFY:** Creator outreach response < 10%, or CAC via paid creators > €5 per activated user.

### A9 — Rules are stable enough to maintain cheaply
- **SUPPORT:** BMG registration deadline long-standing; §16b work limits changed once recently (2024). Tax ID process stable since 2008.
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Blocked-account amounts change yearly; city procedures and URLs change; broadcasting fee amount under political dispute.
- **MITIGATION:** V1 avoids hard-coding changeable amounts; links to source instead.
- **CONFIDENCE:** MEDIUM · **STATUS:** SUPPORTED
- **FALSIFY:** > 20% of rules need changes in a single semester review.

### A10 — Students need offline access
- **SUPPORT:** Arrival involves temporary SIMs/eSIMs; INFERENCE.
- **COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** Most arrive with roaming/eSIM and university Wi-Fi.
- **CONFIDENCE:** LOW · **STATUS:** UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS (cheap to support anyway; local-first chosen for privacy primarily)

### A11 — Generic "worldwide newcomer app" is viable
- **STATUS:** CONTRADICTED by mission constraints and by the variety of rules across corridors (see CORRIDOR_COMPARISON). Rejected.
